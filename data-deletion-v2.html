Data choices and requests

Couples Dare (com.lauri.couplesdare), version 2.0.0 (build 18) and its test builds. Last updated: November 26, 2026. For the earlier version, see previous data choices.

Local game data

The app does not offer dedicated app-account creation or sign-in. There is no Couples Dare account to delete. You can still request deletion of data that we can identify and control. To remove local game progress and preferences, open Android Settings, select Apps, choose Couples Dare, then Storage and Clear storage (labels vary by device). This can remove player names, hearts, streaks, eligibility and telemetry preferences, Signature checkpoints, Midnight Edition collection progress, theme totals, records and other local progress. Android backup or restore features may preserve some data. Clearing storage does not cancel or refund Google Play purchases.

Advertising and Google services

In Settings → Privacy Policy, change the optional usage and crash reporting preference. Use the in-app Ad privacy options where available to manage advertising consent separately. Changing a preference does not delete data already transmitted. Clearing local storage does not by itself delete records held by third-party services such as Google AdMob, Firebase or Google Play. Their data handling is described in the Privacy Policy.

Privacy or data deletion request

Contact Quiz Fans Studio at quizfans.studio@gmail.com. Specify Couples Dare and what data or issue your request concerns. Do not include passwords, payment card details or sensitive information. We will review the request and explain what data we can identify and delete, any applicable retention requirements and relevant third-party options.
